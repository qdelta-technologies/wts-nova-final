- SEO defaults to the verified public origin `https://www.wtsnova.com`. Set `SITE_URL` in hosting only if the canonical domain changes; use the exact public origin including `https://`, never localhost or a preview deployment.
- Submit `https://www.wtsnova.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools after deployment.
- Claim and verify the Google Business Profile for WTS Nova in Nalgonda. Keep the public business name, locality, phone, and website consistent with the site. Add an exact street address, postal code, or hours only if the business publishes and verifies them.
- Structured data describes WTS Nova, its known Nalgonda/Telangana service area, actual services, supplied contact channels, and official profiles. It intentionally contains no invented ratings, hours, street address, or performance promises.
- Search position cannot be guaranteed by metadata. Local visibility also depends on indexing, Business Profile verification, real customer reviews, useful content, and consistent business details across trusted sources.
